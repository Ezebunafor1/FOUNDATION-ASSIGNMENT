    Student Work Spotlight

    website section that highlights and showcases outstanding projects or achievements created by students.

    Modern Residential Building Design

    A student designed a modern residential building focused on comfort, functionality, and visual appeal. The project explored how spaces can be arranged efficiently to meet the needs of a modern family. The design included well-planned bedrooms, living spaces, kitchen areas, and outdoor spaces. The student also considered natural lighting and ventilation. This project demonstrated creativity, architectural planning, and attention to detail.

    Sustainable Building Project

    This project focused on creating a building that is environmentally friendly and energy efficient. The student incorporated natural lighting, proper ventilation, and sustainable construction materials. The design aimed to reduce energy consumption while maintaining comfort for occupants. Green spaces were also included to improve the surrounding environment. The project demonstrated how sustainability can be combined with modern architectural design.

    Structural Engineering Project

    The student developed a structural design for a proposed building with safety and stability as major priorities. The project involved studying foundations, columns, beams, slabs, and other structural elements. Calculations were used to determine suitable structural dimensions and materials. The student also considered the loads that would act on the building. This work demonstrated practical engineering knowledge and problem-solving skills.
